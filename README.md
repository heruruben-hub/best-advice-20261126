# The Best Advice I Ever Got

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[The Best Advice I Ever Got](https://make.fun/c/u-pangg7-the-best-advice-i-ever-got-358cefb0).

## The entry

> My grandfather said never to trust a man who won't look you in the eye when he shakes your hand, and in thirty years I have yet to meet one who proved him wrong.

One sentence, 161 characters. The advice came from the writer's
grandfather, and thirty years of meeting people have yet to prove it
wrong — specific, earned, and true beyond the moment it was given.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the sentence above to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8,
one line, no trailing newline), after checking it against the gate:
1 to 200 characters, not only white space.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
